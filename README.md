# All in One Fintech App

**All in One Fintech App** is a full-service digital banking and investment platform. It lets you manage your money, trade investments, apply for loans and cards, buy crypto, and more — all from one dashboard.

---

## What You Can Do

### Banking
- **Dashboard** – See your total balance across Savings, Investments, and Loans at a glance.
- **Transfer** – Send money to any beneficiary by searching their account number. Choose which account type to debit from.
- **Transactions** – View your full history of credits and debits. Click any transaction to see its details and status.
- **My Cards** – View your virtual Mastercard and apply for a credit or debit card.
- **Loans** – Apply for a loan and track your application status.

### Trading & Investments (FXT)
- **FXT Dashboard** – See your total investment balance, trading profits, referral earnings, and portfolio breakdown.
- **Trade with FXT** – Choose an investment plan (Basic, Standard, Premium, or Special), enter an amount, and start a trade. Watch your profit grow in real-time with a live candlestick chart. Claim your profits when the trade ends.
- **Deposit** – Fund your investment account by sending crypto to one of the available wallet addresses. Use the QR code for easy payment.
- **Withdraw** – Withdraw your investment balance to any crypto wallet.
- **Buy Crypto** – Buy cryptocurrency using bank transfer. Admin-provided account details are shown after you enter your amount.
- **Trade History** – View all your investment and trading transactions in one place.

### Other Features
- **Referral Program** – Share your unique referral link. When someone signs up, you earn **$100** credited directly to your Investments account.
- **Escrow** – Secure escrow services for transactions between users.
- **Profile** – View and edit your personal details, change your password, and manage your account.
- **Stock Ticker** – Live market data displayed across the platform.

---

## Account Types

Your money is organized into three accounts:

| Account | What It's For |
|---|---|
| **Savings** | Everyday banking, transfers, and withdrawals. |
| **Investments** | Trading profits, referral bonuses, and deposit funds. Withdrawals come from here. |
| **Loans** | Tracks any loans you've taken out. |

---

## User Roles

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **User** | Full access to all banking, trading, and profile features. |
| **Investor** | Automatically set when you start a trade. Lets you track and claim your trade profits. Reverts to User when the trade completes. |
| **Moderator** | Same as User, plus access to send funding notification emails. |
| **Admin** | Full access to everything, plus an admin panel to manage users, transactions, investment plans, wallets, and more. Admins cannot trade for security reasons. |

---

## Account Status (Group)

Your account can have one of these statuses:

- **Activated** – Everything works normally. You can transfer, withdraw, and trade.
- **Deactivated** – Some actions are restricted until you verify your account with a code.
- **Cancelled** – Transfers and withdrawals are blocked. Contact support to resolve this.

## Getting Started

1. Visit the website and create an account.
2. Log in to access your dashboard.
3. Explore banking, trading, and other features from the sidebar.
4. To start trading, go to **FXT > Trade with FXT** and choose a plan.
